## Notes

- **C02**: Older records and withdrawn studies often have none. Studies without a start date are left out of start-year counts, never given a made-up year.
- **C03**: Without a date a study cannot appear in the readouts-due view.
- **C04**: The rest are mostly healthy-volunteer Phase 1 studies. Area comes from the condition text, then MeSH, then the molecule (see C15).
- **C06**: Dates come as a day, a month or a year; a month is stored as the first of the month and flagged.
- **C07**: Kept and flagged, left out of timing measures if any appear.
- **C09**: 98 studies outside the pipeline exceed 100,000: observational database studies (one covers 188 million people). They are real, not errors, and are not drug-development studies.
- **C10**: The same study is found by several searches (Merck and MSD); it is kept once.
- **C12**: 37,564 study rows over 44 searches; API data as of 2026-10-14T09:00:05 (unchanged at the end of the download).
- **C13**: Nothing is dropped silently.
- **C14**: Run without --offline to compare against the live API.
- **C15**: Text first, then MeSH, then the molecule's usual area. What no step can place stays Unclassified, never spread across other areas.
- **C16**: Placebo, saline, standard of care and similar comparators are not counted as molecules.
- **C17**: All 14 families match by molecule name, MK development code or brand name.
- **C18**: The registry's MeSH matching is automatic: a lung-cancer study that mentions PD-L1 is also tagged with a Parkinson disease term. Text wins, so those disagreements do not change the area.
- **C19**: The reason is free text and often reads 'see detailed description'. It is mapped by rules, so treat the themes as indicative.
- **C20**: The search is full-text, so names such as 'University of Bristol' come back for 'Bristol'. Each is set aside by an explicit rule; a name that mentions a company and matches no rule is a failure.
- **C21**: Same name, different company: Merck KGaA of Darmstadt owns the name outside the US and Canada.
- **C22**: Cubist, Acceleron, Verona, Prometheus, ArQule and others, under their current owner as of October 2026. Divested or spun-off units (Organon, Alcon, Sandoz) are excluded, not reassigned.
- **C23**: Spellings such as 'Turkey (Türkiye)' and 'Korea, Republic of' are mapped in data/ref/countries.csv.
- **C24**: The snapshot is frozen on purpose so every number on the site and the CV agree.
- **C25**: An overdue study still says it will finish in a month that has gone: the forecast was not updated.
